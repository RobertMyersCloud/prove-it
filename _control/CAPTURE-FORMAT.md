# Prove-It Capture Format

Use this as the default structure for substantial proof artifacts. Small artifacts may combine sections when that improves readability.

## Title
Clear project or investigation name.

## Hiring Claim
> After reviewing this artifact, a hiring manager has evidence that I can ______.

## Employer Skills Demonstrated
List job-relevant skills, tools, and responsibilities demonstrated.

## Scenario / Objective
What problem, investigation, configuration, or capability is being proven?

## Environment
What systems, devices, networks, data, or tools were involved?

## Starting State
What was true before work began?

## Methodology / Work Performed
What did I actually do? Focus on decisions and technical actions rather than dumping every command.

## Evidence
What machine-generated evidence supports the work? Examples: command output, logs, PCAPs, configuration, scripts, queries, screenshots, exported data, hashes, timelines.

## Analysis
What does the evidence mean? Connect evidence to technical conclusions.

## Validation
How did I prove the result? Include expected vs. observed behavior when useful.

## Troubleshooting
When meaningful: **Expected → Observed → Investigated → Root Cause → Change → Validation**

## Findings
What facts are supported by the evidence? Do not claim more than the evidence establishes.

## Security / Business Impact
When applicable, explain why the finding matters technically, operationally, financially, or from a control/risk perspective.

## Recommendations
When applicable, connect recommendations directly to findings.

## What I Learned
What became clearer through the work?

## Evidence Index
List important evidence files and what each proves.

## Evidence Handling
Confirm public artifacts were reviewed and unnecessary sensitive or identifying information was removed.

## Status
**QUEUED / ACTIVE / THIN / PROVEN**
