# Prove-It

This repo is my hands-on proof of the basic foundations. There are no skipped steps, no hand-waving, and no stopping at high-level theory. The goal of this repo is to apply actual hands-on experience, to theory and knowledge that I have gained. If there is something that I don't understand or don't know, I will remove the floorboards to understand the foundations. Before I can understand the who, what, where, when, and why question I must first understand what normal looks like and what a solid foundation looks like and what an unstable foundation looks like.   

The clusters are built in three different steps. The first step is strictly learning step by step instructions, the second step is a mentorship or coaching step and the final step is an unguided and unassisted closed-book step. After all three steps are completed and during the write up phase I will include what a threat detection hunter would have noticed.
